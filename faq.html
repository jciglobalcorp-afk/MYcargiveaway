Is this real? Do people actually win?

Yes. Every giveaway on MyCarGiveaway.com awards a genuine prize to a real winner, selected according to the Official Rules published for that promotion. The current giveaway is a 2000 Mercedes-Benz ML 320 in Charlotte, NC — 264K miles, automatic, clean title, runs and drives, valued at $2,220. We publish winner selection dates and methods up front — nothing happens behind closed doors.

Do I have to pay anything to enter?

Yes — $10 per entry for the current ML320 giveaway. Bundles: 1 entry $10, 3 entries $25, 5 entries $40. Pay via PayPal to ceo@jciglobalcorp.com or via Chime to $Jci-Global-corp, and include your full name, phone, email, and "ML320" in the payment note. Entry period: Nov 8, 2026 – Feb 8, 2027. Drawing date TBA, winner selected by random drawing and announced live on Instagram / Facebook.

Do I have to pay anything to claim my prize?

No. MyCarGiveaway.com will never ask a winner to pay a fee, "processing charge," shipping cost, or tax payment in order to receive a prize. If you're contacted by someone claiming to be from MyCarGiveaway.com asking for payment or financial information to "release" a prize, it isn't us — don't send money, and report it to us directly.

How are winners chosen?

Winners are selected by random drawing from all eligible entries received during the entry period, as described in the Official Rules for that specific giveaway. We don't hand-pick winners.

What are my odds of winning?

Odds depend on the total number of eligible entries received for that giveaway. We don't publish a fixed number in advance because it changes as people enter — but the drawing itself is random among all eligible entries.

Who is eligible to enter?

Generally, legal residents of the United States who are 18 or older (or the age of majority in their state). Some giveaways may exclude specific states due to local law. Eligibility details are always listed in the Official Rules for each promotion.

How do you make money if the prizes are free to win?

Entry fees. The $10 entries for each giveaway fund the prize and the costs of running the giveaway.

How will I know if I win?

Winners are notified directly using the contact information provided at entry (phone or email, as specified in the Official Rules). We also recommend checking the Winners page and your email, including spam/junk folders.

Where can I read the full legal rules?

Every giveaway has its own Official Rules page, covering eligibility, prize value, entry period, and how winners are selected.

I think I saw a fake version of this site or a scam using your name. What do I do?

Please contact us at ceo@jciglobalcorp.com with any details — screenshots, URLs, phone numbers — so we can investigate. MyCarGiveaway.com will never initiate contact asking for payment, gift cards, or sensitive financial information.
